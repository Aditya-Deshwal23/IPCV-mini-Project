A SIMPLE GUIDE TO CLEARER DOCUMENTS

Why does a document photo need processing?

A photograph can be tilted, shaded, noisy, or out of focus. These changes make printed words harder to read, both for people and for a computer.

The scan steps

First, the page is found and straightened. Next, image processing improves brightness and contrast, reduces small noise, and separates dark text from light paper.

What OCR does

Optical Character Recognition, or OCR, looks at letters in an image and produces editable text. Clear, ordinary printed text is usually easier to recognize than decorative writing.
